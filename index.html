01 — FLAGSHIP PROJECT FLUTTER + GROQ API (LLAMA 3.3 70B)

LegalConnect

AI-powered legal assistance & advocate discovery platform

⚠️ THE PROBLEM

People often struggle to identify applicable legal provisions, complaint venues, jurisdiction, and appropriate next steps when facing legal challenges.

💡 THE SOLUTION

An AI-powered legal platform designed to provide structured legal assistance, parse plain-language queries into actionable legal schema, and connect users with relevant legal professionals.

⚡ SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE WORKFLOW
USER QUERY ➔ QUERY PROCESSING ➔ AI / LLM LAYER (LLAMA 3.3 70B) ➔ LEGAL KNOWLEDGE / RETRIEVAL ➔ STRUCTURED LEGAL RESPONSE ➔ RECOMMENDED NEXT STEPS
✓ LLM Integration & NLP: Leveraged Groq API with Llama 3.3 70B to structure plain-language legal queries into standardized schema including case type, applicable legal sections, filing venue, recommended next steps, and estimated fine range.
✓ Legal Knowledge & Retrieval Workflow: Implemented a retrieval pipeline with deterministic rule fallback to guarantee reliable legal guidance and strict informational-use disclaimer safety.
✓ Full-Stack Architecture & Security: Built a cross-platform client in Flutter/Dart backed by a Node.js and Express REST API, secured with JWT authentication, role-based authorization, and Firebase notifications.
